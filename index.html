What is this?

Chuga Chocho is a free, open-source game made for young children (about ages 3–5). It starts simple — a happy train and a crossing gate — and teaches along the way: geography (travel the USA on a map), trains (steam, diesel, electric), and soon counting, colors, and letters. Everything is spoken out loud in English and Polish. There's no way to lose and nothing scary.

🖐️ Play in your browser

Just tap the big green Play Now button above. Nothing to install, no account, works on a tablet.

🏠 Play offline at home

Want it without internet? Download the project, unzip it, and open the play/index.html file. That's it.

🔧 For tinkerers: a real crossing gate

The game can talk to a real physical crossing gate on your home network. Pressing the real button moves the on-screen gate, and the on-screen buttons move the real one. Wiring and firmware instructions are on the way — see the project page.

👩‍💻 For developers

Plain HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SVG — no framework, no build step. Contributions welcome, especially new places, translations, and art.
